Four rules that stop most scams

Never share codes

No bank, police officer or company will ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV or password. Anyone who asks is a scammer.

Pressure means stop

"Within 12 hours", "avoid arrest", "account suspended". Urgency is there to stop you thinking.

Go there yourself

Open the official app or type the website yourself. Call the number on the back of your card, not the one in the message.

Read the real domain

Only the part just before the first single slash counts: in bank.com.verify.xyz the site is verify.xyz.

Common scams right now

Fake delivery fee

An SMS says a parcel is held and asks for a small fee. The payment page steals your card details.

Tell: tiny amount, odd domain, a deadline.

Fake fines and officials

Messages or calls pretending to be police or a government office, claiming a fine or a legal case.

Tell: threats of arrest and payment through a link.

Bank "security" call

A caller says your card is blocked and asks for the code you're about to receive to "verify" you.

Tell: they ask for an OTP. Hang up.

Task and job offers

Easy money for liking videos or rating products. Small payouts at first, then you are asked to deposit money.

Tell: you pay to earn.

Investment and crypto

Guaranteed returns, celebrity endorsements and trading apps you can't withdraw from.

Tell: guaranteed profit.

Prize or lottery

You "won" a draw you never entered and must pay a processing fee or send ID copies.

Tell: pay to collect a prize.

Account takeover

A "friend" asks for the 6-digit code sent to you by mistake. It's your WhatsApp or email login code.

Tell: any request to forward a code.

Fake apps (APK)

A link asks you to install an app to track a parcel or pay a bill. The app reads your SMS and bank codes.

Tell: a download outside the app store.

If you already clicked or paid

  1. Call your bank now using the number on your card. Ask them to block the card and stop or recall the transfer.
  2. Secure your accounts. Change passwords, turn on two-step verification and sign out of other devices.
  3. Uninstall any app the link made you download.
  4. Keep evidence: screenshots, phone numbers, links and transaction references.
  5. Report it to the police and warn your contacts if your account was used.
