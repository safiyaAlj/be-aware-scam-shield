What you send us

When you check a link, message or screenshot on the website, in the browser extension or on WhatsApp, we receive only what you choose to submit. On WhatsApp we also receive your phone number, because WhatsApp needs it to deliver our reply.

How it is checked

Links are compared on our server against known scam patterns, public lists of reported phishing sites (downloaded to our server, so your link is not sent to them) and an automatic model. If enabled, links are also checked with Google Safe Browsing. To see how old a website is, only its main domain name (for example example.com, never the full link) is looked up with the domain's official registry. Messages you ask us to analyze are checked on our server by our own model and rules. Only if the optional AI service (Anthropic) is switched on are messages and screenshots sent to it to produce the assessment; the provider processes the content only to return a result to us.

What we keep

Scam Check does not store the content you submit. Our servers keep technical logs (time, error codes) without message content. [Confirm retention periods and any statistics kept, e.g. counts of scam types for awareness campaigns.]

The browser extension

The extension checks the address of the page you are on inside your browser to show a warning badge. Addresses are not sent to our server unless you choose "Check with كن واعياً" or right-click a link to check it. On Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo results pages it sends the result links (never your search words) to our server to mark risky results; you can turn this off in the extension.

The challenge game

The name you enter in the كن واعياً challenge stays in your browser to show your result. It is not sent to us or anyone else.

Please don't send

Passwords, PINs, OTP codes, full card numbers or ID numbers. Cover them before sending a screenshot.

Questions about scams or this service: call the police on 901 (non-emergency). Emergency: 999. [Add data protection contact.]
